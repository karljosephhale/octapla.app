Octapla privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-29

Octapla is a Bible reader for Android and Windows, published by Inside Out Play, Inc. It is free, has no ads and no account, and we don't collect anything about you.

What stays on your device

Your notes, tags, highlights, links, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device, and nowhere else unless you put them somewhere. We never receive them.

  • Sync. If you turn on sync, Octapla writes to a folder you choose. If that folder is kept in step by a cloud service (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox), that service's own terms apply to it.
  • Export. An export is a file saved where you choose.
  • Android backup. On Android, the system backs up app data to your Google account if you've turned backup on. Octapla leaves its sharing key out of that backup.

When Octapla connects to the internet

Only when you choose a translation that is read online (today, the Hawaii Pidgin Bible), and only after you agree in the app. Octapla then asks the YouVersion Platform, run by Life.Church, for each chapter it shows. YouVersion sees your device's internet (IP) address and which chapters are requested. No notes, names or settings are sent. The text is kept in memory while Octapla is open and never saved on your device. If you untick every online translation, Octapla stops connecting and forgets your yes. YouVersion's privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Otherwise Octapla doesn't connect to anything. Links to sources and licenses open in your own browser.

Sharing a passage

When you share a passage, Octapla makes a link or a file, and you send it with an app you choose. The passage and your notes travel inside the link, after the "#", which browsers don't send to any server. Someone without Octapla can open the link at octapla.app. That page loads only files from octapla.app, uses no cookies or analytics, and stores nothing in the browser. octapla.app is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

Feedback

"Send feedback" and "Report a problem with this verse" open your own mail app with an email to feedback@octapla.app. It contains the app's version and the kind of device, and for a verse report the passage and the translations on screen. You can read and change it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use your email only to read and answer it.

Children

Octapla is not made for children under 13, and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If what Octapla sends ever changes, this page will change first, with a new date.
